Grilled Cheese

Description

Literally just toasted bread and melted cheese!

Ingredients

  • Two slices of bread
  • Two slices of your favorite cheese
  • Butter

Instructions

  1. Use knife to spread butter evenly on one side of each bread
  2. Place cheese in the bread
  3. Toast both sides of the sandwich until golden brown and the cheese is melting
  4. Let cool and enjoy
