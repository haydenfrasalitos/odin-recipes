Quesadilla

Description

Toasted tortilla and melted cheese!

Ingredients

  • Tortilla
  • Your favorite cheese, preferably blended

Instructions

  1. Place one tortilla in a dry skillet over medium heat
  2. Sprinkle (or place) your cheese on top
  3. Fold the tortilla in half
  4. Cook until one side is golden brown, then flip and cook until the cheese melts.
